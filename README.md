### The Classroom Daydream: Desk 14
In October 2021, while teachers droned on through double-period lectures about calculus integrals and coordinate geometry, two students sat at **Desk 14 in the back row** of Section-B. In the margins of their student exercise notebooks, they weren’t copying equations; they were sketching grind distribution charts, calculating Arabica extraction percentages, and drafting recipes for cold extractions. 

That notebook was titled **Notebook Section-B**. From those classroom daydreams grew **SBC (Section-B Coffee)**.

### Why "Section-B"?
Every school and university campus has a Section-B. 
* Section-A was the front-row class: the students who memorized formulas to repeat textbook answers verbatim.
* The back rows of Section-B were for the **curious, restless dreamers**: the ones who questioned why things were done the way they were, who balanced chemistry homework with blueprints for independent ventures, and who believed that great craft requires rebellion.

### The Anti-Pretentious Specialty Coffee Ethos
The specialty coffee movement across Indian metropolitan hubs has often fallen into an unfortunate trap: sterile, clinical white-cube spaces, snobbish barista gatekeeping, astronomical pricing, and an atmosphere that makes students and neighborhood regulars feel judged.

**Section-B Coffee exists as the exact antithesis of that snobbery:**
* **Unpretentious & Collegiate:** It feels like your favorite classroom bench or university study hall — smelling of morning bakes, glowing with warm wooden accents, and buzzing with open conversations.
* **Warmth Over Clinical Minimalism:** Deep forest greens, rich walnut woods, and handwritten student notes replace cold stainless steel and aloof hostility.
* **Zero Rush Sanctuary:** Power sockets at every banquette, quiet study playlists, high-speed WiFi, and never any pressure to clear your table after finishing a cup.

### The 1670 Western Ghats Heritage Lineage
Section-B Coffee does not serve anonymous commercial robusta blends. Every bean is rooted in Indian coffee history:
* In **1670**, the Sufi pilgrim **Baba Budan** made his pilgrimage back from Mecca. Revered for his love of the restorative black broth, he defied the Yemeni monopoly (which boiled or parched seeds under penalty of death) by secretly strapping **seven fertile green coffee beans** against his chest under his travel robes — seven being a sacred number in Sufi philosophy.
* He planted those seven smuggled seeds in the mineral-rich, red laterite soils of the Chandragiri Hills in Karnataka — now honored as the **Baba Budan Giri** range.
* SBC roasts exclusively from certified high-elevation shaded micro-lots in Chikmagalur (1,200m – 1,450m above sea level), where cherries mature slowly under native silver oak, rosewood, and jackfruit canopies, developing deep caramelized sugars and natural sweetness.

### The Chemistry of 204°C First-Crack Roasting
At SBC Labs, roasting is treated as empirical thermodynamics:
* **The Maillard Cascade (150°C – 180°C):** Amino acids and reducing monosaccharides recombine into volatile flavor compounds reminiscent of freshly baked almond frangipane and roasted pecans.
* **The Sweet Spot (204°C / 399°F):** Internal steam fractures the cellular matrix in an audible *First Crack*. We arrest the drum roast within 90 seconds of first crack at precisely 204°C. 
* **The Sensory Payoff:** At this exact temperature, natural sucrose degrades into buttery toffee and cocoa nuances with zero throat bitterness. We never roast dark past second crack because true specialty Arabica needs no ash to hide defects.

### The Sculpted Teddy Bear Latte Ritual
The **Section-B Mascot** — an adventurous collegiate teddy bear in a varsity rugby shirt — was immortalized into our signature drink:
* Baristas hand-freeze an artisanal espresso extract into a detailed three-dimensional teddy bear cube.
* The bear sits peacefully in an ivory ceramic glass. When warm, textured condensed milk and microfoam are poured tableside, the teddy bear slowly and gracefully melts, creating a decadent, naturally sweet iced latte ritual that customers photograph and cherish.

## 3. Technology Stack & Engineering Blueprint

### Why Pure Zero-Dependency Node.js?
In an era where modern web applications suffer from massive `node_modules` bloat (hundreds of megabytes for basic blogs), **Section-B Coffee is engineered for decades of zero-maintenance longevity:**
* **Native Node.js Standard Library:** Uses built-in `http`, `fs`, `path`, and `crypto` modules.
* **Sub-50ms Response Times:** Zero framework startup latency, zero bundler overhead, and immediate execution on any machine running Node.js 18+.
* **100/100 Lighthouse Performance:** No massive client-side React/Vue hydration bundles choking mobile CPU threads.

### Dual-Runtime Engine: Local Server + Vercel Serverless
The core server (`serve.js`) is architected with a dual-personality runtime:
1. **Standalone Local / VPS / Docker Server:** When executed via `node serve.js` or `npm start`, it spins up a high-performance HTTP server listening on `PORT` (default: 3005) with native HTTP range request video streaming and compression.
2. **Vercel Serverless Function:** When imported by `api/index.js`, it exports the asynchronous request handler `module.exports = handleRequest` as a standard Vercel serverless function, allowing instant global edge deployment.

### File-Backed Data Persistence with Serverless Fallbacks
All content, drafts, and reviews are stored as version-controllable JSON documents in the `data/` directory:
* `data/blog_posts.json`: Dynamic journal articles with SEO titles, keywords, reading times, and HTML content.
* `data/reviews.json`: Customer sticky note testimonials with star ratings, student badges, and moderation approval flags.
* `data/gallery.json`: Campus community photos categorized under *Events & Meetups*, *Cafe Vibes*, and *Behind the Bar*.

> [!NOTE]
> **Vercel Read-Only Filesystem Handling:**
> Vercel serverless functions run in a read-only container (except `/tmp`). `serve.js` includes an intelligent **two-tier persistence engine**:
> 1. In local / VPS environments, it writes directly to `data/*.json`.
> 2. In serverless environments (e.g. Vercel), if `data/` is read-only, it automatically falls back to writing to `/tmp/*.json` with an in-memory session cache, preventing 500 errors during admin drafting or review submissions.

### Automated Dynamic SEO & Social Graph Engine
Every single article in the Journal dynamically generates:
* Server-side rendered (SSR) markup readable by all search engine crawlers without JavaScript execution.
* Custom OpenGraph (`og:title`, `og:image`, `og:description`) and Twitter Card tags.
* Google-compliant **JSON-LD Schema Markup** (`BlogPosting`, `Blog`, `LocalBusiness`).
* Auto-updating XML Sitemap at `/sitemap.xml` with `<lastmod>` date stamps.

### Hidden Access Triggers
1. **Secret Keyboard Combination:** Press `Ctrl + Shift + A` (Windows/Linux) or `Cmd + Shift + A` (Mac) anywhere on any page to open the console.
2. **Discreet Footer Mark:** Scroll to the footer of any page and **triple-click** the subtle dot or text `"Built at Desk 14"`.

* **Article Publishing Studio:** Rich textarea supporting headings, paragraphs, blockquotes, and custom slug generation.

* **Community Gallery Manager:** Upload event photographs, assign tags (*Events & Meetups*, *Cafe Vibes*, *Behind the Bar*), or delete outdated media.

## 6. Vercel Deployment Guide

Section-B Coffee is pre-configured for zero-configuration, instant deployment on **[Vercel](https://vercel.com/)**.

### Deploying via Vercel CLI
1. Open PowerShell or Terminal in the project root:
   ```bash
   cd C:\Users\Admin\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\section-b-coffee
   ```
2. Install the Vercel CLI (if not already installed):
   ```bash
   npm i -g vercel
   ```
3. Run the deployment command:
   ```bash
   vercel
   ```
4. Follow the interactive prompts (Accept defaults: `section-b-coffee`, framework preset: `Other`).
5. For production deployment:
   ```bash
   vercel --prod
   ```

### Deploying via GitHub (Automated CI/CD)
1. Initialize a git repository and commit all files:
   ```bash
   git init
   git add .
   git commit -m "feat: complete section-b coffee website with dedicated story, journal & admin"
   ```
2. Create a repository on [GitHub](https://github.com/) and push your code:
   ```bash
   git remote add origin https://github.com/<your-username>/section-b-coffee.git
   git branch -M main
   git push -u origin main
   ```
3. Go to [vercel.com/new](https://vercel.com/new), select your repository, and click **Deploy**. Vercel will automatically detect `vercel.json` and deploy your flagship experience globally.


   * All articles, reviews, and images will immediately be active.

## 8. Data Contracts & API Endpoints

### Authentication
* `POST /api/auth/login`: Accepts `{ username, password }`. Returns `{ success: true, token }` or 401.

### Articles API
* `GET /api/articles`: Returns list of all blog posts (including drafts if queried with `?all=1`).
* `POST /api/articles`: Creates a new article (Requires Bearer token or passkey).
* `PUT /api/articles`: Updates an existing article by `id`.
* `DELETE /api/articles?id=:id`: Deletes an article by `id`.

### Community Reviews API
* `GET /api/reviews`: Returns approved customer reviews (or all reviews if `?all=1`).
* `POST /api/reviews`: Submits a new customer review (Pending moderation by default).
* `PUT /api/reviews`: Approves, edits, or updates review status (`approved: true/false`).
* `DELETE /api/reviews?id=:id`: Deletes a review.

### Campus Gallery API
* `GET /api/gallery`: Returns list of gallery media items with category tags.
* `POST /api/gallery`: Adds a media item with `{ url, caption, category }`.
* `DELETE /api/gallery?id=:id`: Deletes a gallery media item.

### SEO Knowledge Base & FAQs API
* `GET /api/faqs`: Returns list of high-intent search query Q&As (supports optional `?category=:name` filter).
* `POST /api/faqs`: Adds a new SEO question item `{ question, targetQuery, category, answer, annotation, relatedSlug, order }`.
* `PUT /api/faqs/:id`: Updates an existing question by `id`.
* `DELETE /api/faqs/:id`: Permanently deletes an FAQ entry.

### Automated Google FAQPage JSON-LD Schema
During every request to `/journal`, `blog_renderer.js` automatically pulls all published FAQs from `data/faqs.json` (or `/tmp/faqs.json` in serverless) and compiles a Google-compliant `FAQPage` schema into the `<head>`:

```html
<script type="application/ld+json">
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "FAQPage",
  "mainEntity": [
    {
      "@type": "Question",
      "name": "What are the best coffee beans for cold brew and espresso at Section-B?",
      "acceptedAnswer": {
        "@type": "Answer",
        "text": "At Section-B Coffee, the best beans for both cold extraction and espresso are our single-origin shade-grown Arabica lots sourced directly from the Baba Budan Giri hills in Chikmagalur..."
      }
    }
  ]
}
</script>
```
This enables Google's search engine results page (SERP) to render expandable rich snippet accordions directly beneath our search result listing, dramatically increasing CTR (Click-Through Rate).
